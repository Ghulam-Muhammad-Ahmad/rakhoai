# Color Psychology for Rakho AI

Let me think about what your product *actually* signals before picking colors.

---

## What Rakho AI Communicates Emotionally

Your product sits at an unusual emotional intersection:

| Brand Signal | What Color Should Convey |
|---|---|
| **Retention / "keep them"** | Stability, safety, holding |
| **Risk detection** | Alertness without panic |
| **AI intelligence** | Modern, sharp, precise |
| **Tutoring / education** | Trust, growth, warmth |
| **Desi roots** | Cultural warmth without being touristy |
| **B2B SaaS** | Professional, premium, paid-tool credibility |

The challenge: most AI products go **cold blue/purple**, most edtech goes **playful green/orange**, and most desi brands go **saffron/maroon**. You need to blend all three without looking confused.

---

## 🎨 My Top Recommendation: **Deep Teal + Warm Saffron**

### The Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Deep Teal | `#0F766E` | Trust + intelligence + uniqueness |
| **Accent** | Warm Saffron | `#F59E0B` | Desi warmth + alertness |
| **Critical / High Risk** | Coral Red | `#DC2626` | Alarm without aggression |
| **Success / Saved** | Emerald | `#10B981` | Growth, retention working |
| **Neutral Dark** | Charcoal | `#1F2937` | Premium, readable text |
| **Neutral Light** | Cream Off-White | `#FAFAF7` | Soft, not stark white |
| **Background** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean canvas |

### Why This Works Psychologically

**Deep Teal (Primary)**
- Teal = blue (trust, intelligence) + green (growth, education)
- Distinct from the sea of blue SaaS products (Stripe, Linear, Notion)
- Conveys "calm intelligence" — exactly the AI feeling you want
- Premium without being cold

**Warm Saffron (Accent)**
- Subtle desi reference — saffron is sacred and culturally rooted in South Asia
- Color psychology: confidence, energy, attention without panic
- Works as the "do this action" button color
- Visually strong against teal

**Coral Red (High Risk)**
- Pure red = panic and aggression
- Coral red = urgent but human, doesn't feel like an error
- Owners need to feel concerned, not scared
- Same hue HubSpot and Airbnb use — proven to drive action

**Emerald Green (Success)**
- "Student saved" — growth + relief
- Standard SaaS success color
- Pairs naturally with teal primary
